The mode switch — the glass wipe, carrying the capsule

One idea, both ways. A bare pane of the system's glass sweeps across the screen, rim leading, and never rests; the tree swaps while it fully covers; the capsule drops from the top to say where you landed and retracts on its own. Every motion is one transform on one layer.

The timeline (→ Worker; ← Customer is the mirror)

0 msTap "Switch" (the confirm is answered). The pane starts from just off the right edge — rim, specular edge, a soft shadow ahead of it.
0 – 260The pane sweeps left and covers the screen (ease-out). Nothing else moves yet.
180The capsule starts dropping from under the status bar — it lands at 400.
260 – 360Full cover. The tree swaps at 300; the incoming home mounts under the pane and reports its first paint.
360 – 640The pane continues off the left edge, revealing the new home. If the paint signal has not come by 360, the pane waits (never past 400 + the failsafe) — it leaves the moment the home is there.
400 – 1100The capsule holds: a mint dot and the word — "Worker" / "Customer".
1100 – 1300The capsule retracts. Total visible motion 640 ms; the screen is interactive from 640.

Smoothness

Two layers, two properties: the pane's translateX and the capsule's translateY, both Reanimated 4 shared values on the UI thread; the pane rasterized for its 640 ms; the swap a single runOnJS at 300; the reveal gated on the incoming tree's first onLayout (400 ms failsafe); reduced motion → a 240 ms crossfade through the ground with the same capsule. No artwork, no blur animated, no timer the eye can catch.
