Live status on the card

Four ways an assigned job can show where it stands, right in My Jobs, without opening it. All four play the same job: hired, on the way, arrived, working, done. Tap a stage to hold it there.

Your device asks for reduced motion, so the stages don't advance on their own. Tap a stage, or press Playing.

AStage railRECOMMENDED

A four-step rail and one status line. The pattern delivery apps use in lists: readable at a glance, and it works for every stage.

BLive route

Sam's dot moves along a short route to your pin while he's on the way, then it turns into a task bar, then the approval countdown.

CProgress ring

Sam's initials in a ring that fills stage by stage, with the status beside it. The most compact.

DMini timeline

The last step, the current one and the next, sliding up as the job moves. The most detail.

What the card can honestly show

The stageHired, on the way, arrived, working and done come from the job's status and the worker's own route and checklist steps, which the app already has.
The arrival time"About 12 min" shows only while the worker shares live location. Otherwise the card shows the booked window, like "Starts Sat, 8–11 AM".
The money"$110 held" until you approve. It never says paid before the payment is released.
MessageThe chat button sits on the card because messaging opens as soon as someone is hired.
