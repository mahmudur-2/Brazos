From Publish to live

Five ways to go from Review and Publish to the Job live page. Each phone loops: the tap on Publish, the transition, then the live page. The live page is the same in all five; it is the design I propose.

Your device asks for reduced motion, so the phones start paused. Press Pause again to play them.

ALift-off

The rocket in Publish takes off, a mint glow rises, and the live page fades in.

BCard glide

Everything else fades while your summary card glides up and becomes the published card.

CSealRECOMMENDED

Publish shrinks to a spinner while the job saves, ticks, then opens the live page from that tick.

DBroadcast

Rings pulse out from Publish and light up workers nearby, then the live page settles.

EFlip

The summary card turns over to show the published card, and the page builds around it.

Why the live page looks like this

The same card you reviewedTitle, category, area, timing and budget in the Review summary's layout, so it reads as the same job. The price is white.
What happens nextBids arrive, you choose and we hold the payment, you approve and they're paid. The money question is answered before it's asked.
Buttons stay putThe bar keeps its place from Review: Back to Home where Save draft was, View job and bids where Publish was.
